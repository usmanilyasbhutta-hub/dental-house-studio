## Logo version control
User fixed the permanent master on 4 October 2026. Use the CURRENT PERMANENT LOGO row and DESIGN.md for every new logo placement. Orange/copper logo variants are obsolete. The older remove-background PNG and historical exports are not the master; do not select them automatically or use them as an unverified substitute. Recover the current master from its persistent reference if the local JPEG is missing. Do not overwrite the master with a generated or recoloured derivative.
