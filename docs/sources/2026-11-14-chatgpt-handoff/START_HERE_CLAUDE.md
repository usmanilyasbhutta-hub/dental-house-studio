## Critical recent owner feedback (November 2026; not guaranteed incorporated into the files)
- Understand what the user wants changed, what the viewer must understand, and what must stay real. Translate that into direction, implement, inspect frames/export and revise.
- Prefer action-driven visual storytelling over explanations or generic caption/title cards. Motion, masking, 3D, compositing, kinetic typography and sound should be selected purposefully per shot.
- Authentic patient/clinic assets and original footage must remain credible; do not invent clinical proof or change identity.
- For dental procedural animations, instrument geometry, real-world scale, working tip, operating angles, motion, material consistency and sequence must be clinically accurate. Reference genuine equipment before modeling. Check continuity and contact with actual teeth, gingiva and materials.
- Physically implausible props, made-up instruments, unnatural transitions, visible composites and AI-looking results are defects, even if technically generated.
- Approved elements remain unchanged unless owner explicitly requests a revision.
- Review exported frames and actual motion rather than asserting that effects were implemented.
