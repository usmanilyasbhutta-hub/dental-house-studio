Sound and type

MusicOriginal, made in code. To suit the lighter look I would swap the dholki groove for something softer: santoor-style plucks over a warm pad with a light tabla pattern, about 92 bpm. Mixed to −14 LUFS.
TypeBold sentence-case sans for headlines, as in Carousel V2, with one bronze serif italic word per page. This replaces the all-caps condensed type the studio normally uses, for this video only unless you say otherwise.

Two choices for you

A or B: headline styleA: bold sans like Carousel V2 Premium (drawn here). B: bold serif like the Assessment Carousel (“Wedding + WHITENING”).
A or B: musicA: soft santoor and tabla (planned here). B: keep the livelier dholki from the first draft.
