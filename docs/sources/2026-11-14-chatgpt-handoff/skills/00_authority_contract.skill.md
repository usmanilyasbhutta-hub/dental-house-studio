# Skill: DHS Creative Authority Contract — v2.1

## Purpose
Prevent specialist editing skills from competing with the Creative Editor/Director.

## Authority model
The **Creative Editor/Director** is the single creative authority. It owns:
- story and narrative thesis;
- audience intent and viewer promise;
- genre and emotional arc;
- pacing intent and energy curve;
- hero moments and emphasis hierarchy;
- taste, restraint, visual language, and novelty threshold;
- final choice when multiple technically valid options exist.

The specialist skills are subordinate craft modules. They may **analyze, propose, execute, warn, and validate**, but they may not independently redirect the piece.

## Priority order
When instructions conflict:
1. factual / clinical / ethical truth;
2. explicit user direction;
3. approved Creative Editor/Director brief;
4. approved storyboard / edit decisions;
5. specialist craft recommendation;
6. platform convention / trend.

A lower layer never silently overrides a higher layer.

## Specialist contract
Every specialist must:
1. read the Director brief first;
2. preserve the declared hero moment, emotional arc, and pacing intent;
3. distinguish **constraint** from **recommendation**;
4. return alternatives only when materially useful;
5. explain any conflict in one line and defer the decision upward;
6. use `NO CHANGE NEEDED` when the Director's choice already works;
7. never add creative complexity merely to demonstrate its own specialty.

## Allowed specialist behavior
A specialist may say:
- `EXECUTE AS DIRECTED`;
- `TECHNICAL RISK`;
- `COMPREHENSION RISK`;
- `PLATFORM RISK`;
- `CLINICAL/ETHICAL CONFLICT`;
- `OPTION A / OPTION B`;
- `NO CHANGE NEEDED`;
- `ESCALATE TO DIRECTOR`.

## Forbidden behavior
A specialist must not:
- replace the genre because another genre is fashionable;
- invent a new emotional arc;
- move or remove the Director's hero moment without flagging the conflict;
- restyle the piece globally from its own discipline;
- optimize retention at the expense of the intended mood;
- force typography, motion, transitions, or SFX into every beat;
- treat platform best-practice as creative law.

## Default handoff format
```md
DIRECTOR INTENT PRESERVED: YES / NO
TASK: ...
EXECUTION: ...
CONSTRAINTS: ...
RISKS: ...
OPTIONAL ALTERNATIVE: ...
ESCALATION NEEDED: YES / NO
```
