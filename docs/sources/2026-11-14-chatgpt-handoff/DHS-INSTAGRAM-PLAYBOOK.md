# DHS Instagram playbook
Effective 10 October 2026. Read the current Dental_House_30_Day_Hormozi_Plan.md for strategy/calendar; WORKFLOW.md for execution; DESIGN.md and ASSETS.md for current identity. User-approved refinements override defaults.

## Audience and promise
Local patients considering dental care; parents/caregivers and adults comparing options. Diagnosis First: understand findings and choices before treatment. Recurring benefit: clear dental answers, actual clinic explanations and practical preparation. Natural Urdu/Roman Urdu with relevant English terms. Faceless default; do not require the owner on camera.

## Content and distribution
Two useful Reels + one save-worthy carousel per week; 3–5 relevant Story days. These are DHS workload choices, not algorithm requirements. One specific question, useful payoff, authentic proof and one action.
Three series: Before You Decide; Dental Question, Clear Answer; Inside Dental House.
Prioritise actual clinic/models/process/community footage over abstract trust graphics. Do not show records/people without usable permission and appropriate de-identification.
Prepare partner sharing/co-posts only with a verified partner account and separate authorization. No unsolicited messaging or bought engagement.
Profile/pinned-post changes in the strategy are proposals; verify current state before editing.

## Production settings
Reel: default 1080x1920,9:16; legible first frame and subtitles; keep key text clear of controls and preview feed/profile crop; highest quality upload. For new-audience discovery, keep within 3 minutes under checked official guidance; short one-idea explainers are usually 15–25s as a test, not a mandate.
Carousel/photo: 1080x1350,4:5 production default; 3:4 if composition and route preview support it. Consistent slide ratio. 5–7 useful slides when warranted. Strong cover and second slide; alt text describes actual visuals.
Story:1080x1920;1–3 sequenced frames when useful; public campaign audience; appropriate question/poll/link/location sticker. Check native versus scheduler support.
Music: optional; rights/availability verified; speech clarity first; no double soundtrack. Verify realistic AI audio/video disclosures where required.
Originality, account status and recommendation eligibility checked; eligibility is not a guarantee.
Caption: relevant topic early, meaningful context and one action. Up to five relevant feed/Reel hashtags under checked December2025 guidance; never force all five. No keyword stuffing or inaccurate locations.
